# NeoKeys: product one-pager

**What it is:** Register keyboard shortcuts once, get a help overlay free

**Who it is for:** The person maintaining a Neorgon site who is about to add a
keyboard shortcut, and who has no way of knowing that eight other sites already
use the key they are about to take.

**Register:** product (a tool someone uses), not brand (a page someone visits).

**The scene:** A second monitor, mid-afternoon, with the site's own tab open
next to the editor. They press a key on this page to see what happens, read the
verdict, then copy three lines into their own `events.js`. They are not
browsing; they are checking something before they commit it.

**What done looks like:** They leave with `js/neokeys/` copied into their site
and their own shortcuts registered, or they leave knowing which key is safe to
take. Either counts. The failure is leaving unsure whether their `E` collides
with anything.
